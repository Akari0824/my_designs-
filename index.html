Our Story

A decade in pursuit of the perfect cup.

Ember & Oak began in 2014 with one second-hand drum roaster, two stubborn founders, and a belief that great coffee is worth slowing down for.

We source directly from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — paying well above commodity prices because the people who grow our coffee deserve it.
